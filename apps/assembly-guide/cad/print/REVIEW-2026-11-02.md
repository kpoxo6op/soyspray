## Failure and correction

Ventilation cutters removed the roots of the device locating rims. The failed
drive tray had 551 mm of overhanging wall on layer 21, including a 55.4 mm line
anchored at only one end. The old switch shelf had 214 mm of the same defect.
The slicer's floating-cantilever warning was incorrectly accepted, and the
preview omitted the failing rim layers. Passing the first layer did not justify
continuing the print. The pause at layer 40 came after the rims had finished;
cancelling also lost the chance to inspect the later window bridges.

The fix retains floor material beneath the rim footprints, with a 0.8 mm margin.
An independent layer scan found no floating rim starts in either fixed shelf.
Bounds, mounting holes and device clearances are unchanged. Vent area changes
from 9764 to 9311 mm² for the tray and 8804 to 8610 mm² for the switch shelf.

The front windows still have approximately 37 mm bridges anchored at both ends.
Their G-code speed is **50 mm/s**, with 90% fan. The earlier high-effort review's
10 mm/s claim was incorrect. Sag and strength have not been physically measured.

## Preflight and evidence

`machine.json`, `process.json` and `filament.json` are the exported P1S 0.4 mm,
0.2 mm layer, Creality PETG profiles used for the review: 240°C nozzle, 75°C
textured PEI, four walls, five top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid and 8 mm³/s maximum
flow. They contain no printer connection credentials.

From `cad/`, install renderer dependencies with `npm ci`, then run:

```sh
node preflight.mjs ~/prints/soyrack-new-job drive_tray switch_shelf
```

Use a new output directory for every run. It records the actual generated job
hashes, source and tool hashes, commit and profiles. A renderer or slicer failure
cannot produce a successful report. Check `pass: true` and the exact file hash
before uploading. The general printer API does not yet enforce this contract.

Real renderer-to-slicer checks exercised the original bad shelves (both reject),
fixed shelves (both pass), and a stale source/STL combination (reject). Existing
app checks do not own this contract: they validate the guide and build, not
manufacturing support. No unit or mock tests were added for this fix.
The repeatable boundary check is `python3 check-preflight.py <new-check-dir>`;
its results and full slicer logs stay in that directory. It also exercises
renderer/slicer startup failures, verifies the exported archive against the
inspected G-code, and rejects output-directory reuse without modifying old jobs.
